WHY PRESERVED GENERATION?

Faith doesn’t have to pause because of work.

We want to create a safe, intentional space where young people can meet God, meet people, and not lose themselves in the busyness of life.

01

Connect

Meet other youth, network, and build godly relationships beyond your usual circle.

02

Grow

Connect with God through prayer, movies, dates, seminars, and discussions that keep your faith alive.

03

De-stress

Release work stress, be refreshed, and be reminded that God is still with you in every season.

OUR COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

Come confidently.
Leave transformed.

01 — Christ first
Everything we do points us back to Christ and a deeper fellowship with Him.

02 — Respect everyone
No harassment, discrimination or intentional harm. We’re all growing together.

03 — Dress confidently
Come well-dressed to network well. Let your appearance reflect excellence.

04 — Keep it clean
Let’s keep our space clean and honorable.

05 — Be punctual
Value time. Value people. Value God.

06 — Participate
Come ready to listen, encourage and contribute.
